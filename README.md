 🌍 ORACLE ATLAS

# The World's Story Through Numbers.

*Oracle Atlas* is an interactive global intelligence and data visualization platform designed to transform complex international datasets into immersive, understandable experiences.

Rather than navigating spreadsheets, statistical reports, and disconnected databases, users can explore the world through an interactive 3D Earth, discover individual nations, and understand their development through meaningful visualizations.

> **Consult Civilization. Experience Data. Explore Humanity.**

---

## ✨ The Vision

Global organizations such as the World Bank and United Nations maintain extensive repositories of information about countries, economies, populations, sustainability, and social development.

However, accessing and interpreting this information often requires navigating complex datasets and statistical interfaces.

Oracle Atlas aims to bridge this gap by combining **geospatial visualization, data analytics, interactive design, and technology** into a unified platform.

The objective is simple: make global data accessible, engaging, and meaningful to everyone.

## 🪐 Core Features

### 1. The Oracle Experience

- Immersive landing page with a cosmic-inspired visual identity.
- Floating Greek mathematical symbols and ambient animations.
- Interactive entry experience through the *Consult the Oracle* interface.

### 2. Interactive 3D Earth

- Satellite-inspired, interactive 3D globe.
- Real-time rotation and geographical exploration.
- Country identification through hover interactions.
- Country names and national flags.
- Cinematic geographical transitions upon country selection.

### 3. The Four Pillars of Civilization

Each country is explored through four fundamental dimensions:

| Pillar         | Indicator      | Data Focus                                  |
| -------------- | -------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| **PROSPERITY** | GDP            | Economic performance and growth             |
| **HUMANITY**   | Population     | Demographics and population trends          |
| **HARMONY**    | SDG Indicators | Sustainable development and social progress |
| **STABILITY**  | Crime & Safety | Public safety and crime-related indicators  |

### 4. Experiential Data Visualization

- Interactive charts and graphical representations.
- Colorful, dynamic visual storytelling.
- Contextual explanations of statistical indicators.
- Historical comparisons wherever data is available.

### 5. Expandable Intelligence

The platform is designed with a modular architecture, allowing additional indicators, datasets, and analytical capabilities to be integrated over time.

## 📊 Data Sources

Oracle Atlas aims to utilize publicly available and authoritative international datasets.

- **World Bank Open Data:** Economic indicators, GDP, population, and development statistics.
  [https://data.worldbank.org/](https://data.worldbank.org/)

- **United Nations Sustainable Development Goals:** Global sustainability indicators and progress data.
  [https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/](https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/)

- **REST Countries:** Country metadata, geographical information, and national flags.
  [https://restcountries.com/](https://restcountries.com/)

- **Additional Open Datasets:** Supplementary sources for crime, public safety, and other social indicators.

*Data availability, reporting periods, definitions, and geographical coverage may differ across sources. Each indicator should retain its source and reporting year.*
